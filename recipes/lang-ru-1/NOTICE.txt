Russian language lists for reading aloud: where the letter yo belongs (yo.txt.gz), stress of common words (stress.txt.gz), context rules for words spelled alike (homographs.tsv.gz), and a small network that guesses stress for words in no list (stress-net.bin with its letters and its hidden-yo list). Text lists are gzip.
