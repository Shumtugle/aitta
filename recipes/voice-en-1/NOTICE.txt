Speech model for English (text to speech), the English half of the reading voice.
It shares the encoder and decoder of the Russian voice piece and is used beside it.

The model is Pocket TTS by Kyutai Labs, licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Pocket TTS was created by Manu Orsini, Simon Rouard, Gabriel De Marmiesse,
Vaclav Volhejn, Neil Zeghidour and Alexandre Defossez.
https://github.com/kyutai-labs/pocket-tts

Changes: the model was exported to ONNX and its weights quantized to 8-bit integers
for on-device use; state inputs and outputs were widened to 32-bit floats.
The weights were not retrained.

The model must not be used to clone or impersonate a voice without consent,
to mislead, or for any harmful or unlawful purpose.
